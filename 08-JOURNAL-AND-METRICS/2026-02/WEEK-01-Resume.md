## 1️⃣ Strategy & Cadence (Reference Model)

This roadmap follows a **compressed weekend model** to preserve social balance and long-term sustainability.

- Weekdays (Mon–Fri): full technical days
- Weekend (Sat + Sun): counted as **one single engineering day**
- One weekend day intentionally freed for social and family obligations
- Objective: consistency over burnout

This structure will be reused across all weeks.
